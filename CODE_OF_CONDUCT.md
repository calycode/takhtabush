# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Takhtabush to be a place where people can learn, ask questions, and
disagree about code without being harassed. We pledge to make participation
welcoming regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being direct about code and kind about people.
- Giving and accepting specific, actionable feedback.
- Assuming good faith, then asking a clarifying question.
- Respecting that maintainers have limited time.
- Crediting other people's work.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory comments.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions, or reviews.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any
space where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer at **t.mihaly@icloud.com**.
Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may remove comments, close
threads, or block accounts, and will explain the decision where it is safe to
do so.
