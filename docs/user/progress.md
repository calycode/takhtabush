# Progress

Open a deck and choose **See progress**, or use the statistics link from the
dashboard.

## What the cards mean

| Metric   | Meaning                                           |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Accuracy | Correct reviews divided by all reviews            |
| Due now  | Cards whose next review is today or overdue       |
| Streak   | Consecutive days with at least one review         |
| New      | Cards you have never reviewed                     |
| Learning | Cards that are in the schedule but not yet mature |
| Reviews  | Total reviews recorded                            |

## Current schedule

The schedule table shows how many cards come back on each of the next two
weeks. Use it to plan a short session: a day with many due cards is a good day
for **Review**, while an empty day is a good day for **Learn**.

## Card accuracy

The table lists cards from the weakest to the strongest. A card with a low
accuracy or several lapses is a good candidate for **Diagnose** or **Revise
now**.

## What to study next

- **Many due cards** — use the Review goal.
- **A few weak cards** — use Diagnose or Revise now.
- **New deck** — use Learn with a short timer.
- **Exam soon** — use Revise now to ignore the schedule and cover the set.

The statistics page is read-only; it never changes your schedule. Studying is
what moves the numbers. The streak is cached for up to ten minutes, so it can
lag a review that just happened and catches up on its own.
