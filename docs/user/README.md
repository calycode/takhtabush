## The five-minute version

1. Sign in with your email; open the link you receive.
2. Choose **New deck**, name it, and save.
3. Open the deck and choose **Add card**.
4. Pick a card type, write the front and back, and save.
5. Choose **Study**, pick a mode and a time limit, and start.

Your dashboard shows decks, total cards, and any sessions you paused. The
statistics page shows what is due and which cards are giving you trouble.
